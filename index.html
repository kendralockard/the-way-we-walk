the way we walk

welcome

What I have to offer is a long walk. I see this life as a long walk. And the only way it has ever made sense to me has been to walk with the trees and the mountains and the oceans and the birds and each other.

This is what I would like to give: I would like to share the space, the opportunity and the structure to walk with the more-than-human beings of this Earth. I would like to teach all that I can around feeling more at home with this Earth and thus more at home with ourselves.

about me

Avalon Qian

I have been working in the outdoor industry since 2013 and it has been primarily in outdoor education. I have led and instructed people in the mountains of California, Wyoming, Alaska and many other places. My work recently culminated in me giving myself a month long solo journey in the Grand Canyon. I wish to share my work with all who are interested. I am a certified Wilderness First Responder. This is a certification received after taking an 80 hour course that teaches how to respond to emergency medical situations in remote settings.

I believe in the work of standing with ourselves, feeling the Earth under our feet and breathing. That’s it. I believe in slowing down so we can hear ourselves and the birds. I think the Earth wants to be listened to and I hope we can be a part of this healing.
