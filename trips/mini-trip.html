mini trip for young ones
(ages 5-10)

a week full of day trips exploring the Bay Area

when
June 29 – July 3
where
surrounding Bay Area
cost
sliding scale $600–$1200*

On our days together we will explore the nooks and crannies of the Bay Area. We will dip our toes into salt water, find hidden corners of the city, explore fairy forests, speak with the birds and the plants, we will spend time listening, playing and exploring what exists on our every day walks.

Locations each day will be subject to change depending on weather, participant ability and the resources at our disposal. Each day parents and guardians will receive an email with information about where we will be going the next day and what kind of clothes / food their youth should be prepared with.

Pick up and drop-off locations will be determined based on participant living locations and where they will be driving from. The aim will be to choose a location that is relatively equidistant to all of us.

*If transportation or cost is a barrier for you, feel free to email WayWeWalk@proton.me to discuss potential alternatives.
