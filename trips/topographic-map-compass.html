topographic map and compass navigation class

come and learn how to situate yourself in relationship to the land using a topographic map and compass

when

9am–3pm
where
Marin Headlands
cost
$100

In this class we will use a map and compass to navigate through varied terrain, learning how to use the features around us rather than just a trail.

We will become competent navigators by learning to situate ourselves to the cardinal directions, to the magnetic field of the Earth, to the Sun, among the vegetation, among the dips and valleys forged by water, to each other, and more.

We will spend less time following a trail and more time understanding how to map ourselves in relation to the ridges, peaks and valleys.

This is a class for anyone who wants to become more competent at the technical skills of reading a topographic map and compass while simultaneously connecting to the land.

To know where you are in space is to take further agency of your own body, life and time on this planet. Know where you are.
